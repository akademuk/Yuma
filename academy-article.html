Innovations in Aesthetic Medicine 2025

Event Goal:

"Research, Technology, and Practice: Discover New Horizons in Aesthetic Medicine with Experts"

Date & Time:

December 20–21, 2025, 10:00 – 18:00

Location:

Online Platform + In-person at "HealthTech Hub" Conference Center, Kyiv, Ukraine

REGISTER NOW

Event Description

This two-day conference, "Innovations in Aesthetic Medicine 2025," is dedicated to modern approaches in dermatology, cosmetology, and medical aesthetics. The program includes lectures by leading experts, practical masterclasses, and interactive sessions discussing the latest products and technologies.

The goal of the event is to familiarize participants with the latest scientific research, clinical trials, and practical case studies, as well as to provide an opportunity to exchange experiences with colleagues from different countries.

Who Should Attend

This event is valuable for anyone looking to simplify their workflow and boost professional efficiency.

Dermatologists and Cosmetologists

  • Learn about the latest skin regeneration and care methods.
  • Gain practical case studies on the use of fillers, boosters, and peels.
  • Opportunity to discuss clinical protocols and safe product application practices.

Clinic Owners and Managers

  • Learn how to integrate new products and technologies into your practice.
  • Receive advice on optimizing clinic operations and increasing procedure efficiency.
  • Opportunity to find partners and establish professional connections.

Research and Development (R&D) Specialists

  • Evaluate new methodologies for clinical testing and research.
  • Learn about the results of the latest scientific developments in aesthetic medicine.
  • Opportunity to exchange experiences with international experts.

Students and Young Specialists

  • Gain valuable knowledge about modern trends and practical methods in dermatology and cosmetology.
  • Opportunity to familiarize yourself with real case studies and clinical research results.
  • First experience communicating with leading industry experts.

Speakers & Experts

The event features leading experts in aesthetic medicine, dermatology, and clinical research. Combining years of practical experience with scientific expertise, they present the most current methodologies, innovations, and trends in medicine and cosmetology. Speakers will discuss modern approaches to skin regeneration, the use of fillers and boosters, and share their findings from clinical trials and product efficacy assessments.

Participants will have a unique opportunity to review practical case studies, ask questions directly to the experts, and discuss new ideas with peers. Additionally, the event features interactive sessions and panel discussions designed to immerse you in the latest technologies and approaches in aesthetic medicine. This is an excellent opportunity not only to gain knowledge but also to expand your professional network and discover new directions for growing your practice.
